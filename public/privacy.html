Privacy notice

Last updated: 16 October 2026

ChériChef is a small cookbook for couples and their friends. This notice explains, in plain words, what we keep about you, why, who helps us run the service, and how you can see, fix or delete it.

The short version
  • We keep what you put into ChériChef, plus the email address you sign in with.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking cookies, and we never sell your data.
  • You can delete your account yourself, at any time, from your profile.

Who is responsible

ChériChef is run by an individual based in the European Union, who is the controller of your personal data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). For anything about your data, write to [CONTACT EMAIL].

Our legal basis

  • Providing the service you signed up for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)): your account and everything you add to it.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): keeping the service secure, short-lived logs, backups, and handling reports so the club stays a kind place.

Who helps us run ChériChef

We use a few providers ("processors") who handle data only on our instructions, under data processing agreements:

  • Oracle Cloud Infrastructure — rents us the server that runs the website, the database, sign-in and photo storage. The software (Coolify and Supabase) is open source and runs on that server, so your data is stored only there, in Oracle's Frankfurt, Germany data centre (EU).
  • Google (Gmail) — sends account emails such as sign-up confirmations and password resets. Google receives your email address and the message.

We do not use analytics, advertising or social-media trackers, and we do not sell or rent your data to anyone.

Cookies and storage on your device

ChériChef sets no cookies. Your browser's local storage keeps your sign-in session so you stay signed in, and signing out removes it. The offline preview kitchen also saves your sample changes in local storage on that device only. Because nothing is used for tracking, there is no cookie banner.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and content are kept until you delete your account.
  • Encrypted backups of the database are kept for up to 90 days, then deleted. Deleted data disappears from backups as they expire.
  • Photos uploaded but never saved to a recipe or cover are deleted automatically after about a week.
  • Hosting logs are kept only briefly by our providers.
  • Reports are kept while needed to handle them. Reports you sent are deleted with your account.

Deleting your account

Open your profile and choose Delete my account. You confirm with your username and current password, and then:

  • Cookbooks only you keep are deleted, with their recipes, photos and reviews.
  • A cookbook you share with a partner stays with them. If you own it, your partner becomes its owner. Your recipes and photos in it stay, shown as written by a "Former member", because they are part of a cookbook you built together.
  • Pending invitations to your cookbooks are cancelled.
  • Your profile, reviews, replies, follows, friendships and reports are removed.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. If you want recipes you added to a shared cookbook removed too, delete them before you leave, or ask us.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access your data and receive a copy, correct it (you can edit your profile, cookbooks and recipes yourself), erase it (delete your account in the app, or ask us), restrict or object to how we use it, and take it with you (portability). Write to [CONTACT EMAIL] and we will reply within one month. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Reporting content

You can report a profile, cookbook, recipe, review or reply that is not yours. A report records who sent it, what it is about, a reason and an optional note. Only the person who runs ChériChef reads reports, and may remove content or accounts as described in the terms.

Children

ChériChef is meant for people aged 16 and over.

Changes

If this notice changes in a way that matters, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, let members know in the app.
